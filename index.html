Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CBI and RBI?

While both Citizenship by Investment (CBI) and Residency by Investment (RBI) offer structured, legal pathways to enhance your global mobility and secure your wealth, they serve different strategic goals, offer distinct legal rights, and operate on vastly different timelines.

Citizenship by Investment (CBI): The Direct Pathway to a Second Passport

  • What it is: CBI is a streamlined process that grants you full nationality and a second passport directly in exchange for a qualifying economic contribution to a host country. Common investment routes include nonrefundable state contributions, real estate purchases, or national development fund investments.
  • The Benefits: You receive the exact same rights as any other citizen of that nation. This includes the right to live and work indefinitely, vote in national elections (in certain jurisdictions), hold public office, and seamlessly pass your citizenship down to future generations.
  • Timeline and Cost: CBI programs are designed for speed and efficiency, entirely bypassing traditional naturalization requirements. Citizenship is often granted within 3 to 18 months, with minimum investments typically starting from USD 100,000.

Residency by Investment (RBI): The "Golden Visa" Lifestyle Pathway

  • What it is: Often referred to as a "Golden Visa," RBI provides a residence permit that grants you and your family the legal right to live, work, and study in the host country.
  • The Benefits: RBI is ideal for individuals seeking a physical lifestyle change, enhanced structural tax efficiency through relocation, or strategic access to regional markets (such as the European Union's Schengen Area). While it provides an immediate safe haven and access to premium healthcare and education, it does not immediately confer full citizenship rights, such as holding a passport or voting.
  • Timeline and Cost: RBI programs generally offer lower initial investment thresholds, with some starting from USD 25,000. While you do not receive a passport immediately, most RBI programs offer a clear pathway to naturalized citizenship. This usually requires holding the legal residency for 5 to 10 years and meeting specific physical presence or language criteria.

Summary: If your priority is immediate, unrestricted global mobility and securing a permanent generational legacy without the need to relocate, CBI is the definitive choice. However, if your goal is to physically relocate your family, establish tax residency in a favorable jurisdiction, or build a gradual pathway to citizenship over several years, an RBI program provides the perfect foundation.

Are these programs legal and officially recognized?
Absolutely. Investment migration in 2026 has evolved into a highly structured, institutionalized sector of international law. We strictly facilitate government approved, legislated pathways that are firmly embedded in a host nation’s constitution or immigration framework. Unlike discretionary migration routes, these Residence and Citizenship by Investment (RBI/CBI) programs are explicitly designed by sovereign states to attract foreign direct investment. This guarantees that your acquired rights—whether a second passport or a European residence permit—are legally undisputed, offering absolute regulatory compliance and unbreachable security for your assets.
Can I include my family members?
Yes. Modern wealth strategy is fundamentally about generational resilience, and the most robust programs are explicitly designed for comprehensive, multigenerational inclusion. Depending on the specific jurisdiction, a single strategic application can typically encompass your spouse, dependent children, and frequently your dependent parents or grandparents. This ensures that your entire family secures an immediate safe haven, uninterrupted access to premium global healthcare and education, and a permanent legacy of unrestricted mobility that can be seamlessly inherited by your descendants.
Do I need to relocate to obtain the visa/passport?
In the vast majority of cases, no. A strategic "Plan B" is engineered to provide geographic optionality without disrupting your current global operations or lifestyle. Top tier Caribbean Citizenship by Investment programs typically require zero physical presence, granting you a passport directly. Similarly, highly sought after European Residency by Investment programs offer extreme flexibility; for example, Hungary mandates absolutely no minimum physical stay to maintain its 10 year residence permit, while Portugal requires an average of just seven days per year. This allows you to legally institutionalize a highly secure fallback position while maintaining total control over where you actually choose to live.
